# Style directions

The style briefs for the gallery, plus `00-baseline` as the control. Each pairs a **visual style** with the **conversion hook** it suits best. The two axes are independent, so a hook can be reused or swapped.

## Baseline observations (live page, Oct 2026)

- Headline "Welcome to Board Game Arena!" names the site but sells nothing. The real hook ("largest boardgame table in the world") sits below it.
- Strong proof (10.8M players, 1,396 games, 8.95M games/month, 42 languages) is presented as plain text instead of a hero moment.
- The 200+ game carousel is a wall, with no curation for a newcomer choosing a first game.
- "Start playing now" is the only CTA. Nothing speaks to the most common reason people arrive: a friend invited them.
- Minimal, functional visuals carry little of the warmth of a physical game night.

## Styles

The gallery holds twenty-five styles. 01–03 are themed looks; 04–10 are each drawn from one game in the top ten below, using that game's scenes, props, cards and components. 11–13 are Board Path variants: 03's printed board without its pawns, the winding roadmap track from 03's first iteration, and 01's pick-your-vibe selector. 14–15 keep 11's crossroads fork and its animations but restyle the road and redraw "house rules" and "how to join", borrowing the Sunday-newspaper-comics idiom of the retired `05-funnies-strip` (commit bec3266): banner section headers, comic-strip steps and a box-score table. 16–17 push the drawing itself: both use a boiling-ink flat-colour cartoon technique taken from a reference frame the user supplied (kept out of the repo, see `.gitignore`). As everywhere in this gallery, only the technique is borrowed — every character, name and word on the page is original, and no show, character or publisher is ever named or depicted. 18–20 all build on 14 and share its hero wording — h1 "Welcome to Board Game Arena!" over "Join the largest boardgame table in the world", the live page's own headline and hook — with the three big numbers promoted to a hero moment, and each gives the starting point, "how to join" and "house rules" its own treatment. 11–23 all use 09's type: Rye for the display moments (h1, CTA label, closing heading) and Libre Baskerville for everything else. Style 24 takes 05\u2019s type and palette instead. Earlier rounds (Cartoon Network shows, more *Game of Thrones* takes) were retired; only Hall of Banners survives, renumbered from 08 to 01.

Reference shows, genres and games are for our reference only. Game names may appear (they are the top ten), but publisher logos, title lettering, box art and card art never do in a style's own artwork: draw every component in your own hand. The one exception is styles 21–23, which show the real box covers of the top ten, saved from Board Game Arena into `img/covers/` at the owner's explicit request. Show names, characters, houses, sigils, place names, publishers, logos, catchphrases and title lettering never appear on the page. Evoke the *craft, palette and mood*; every character, shape and word is original. No violence or gore.

**The through-line is playing together.** Every style is an invitation: copy speaks to "you and your friends", "your crew", "pull up a seat", "we saved you a spot"; art shows groups gathered around a game, an empty seat waiting, friends waving you over, hands reaching into the same pile of pieces. No lone hero. Solo-play claims are fine but never the lead.

## Conversion hooks

- **Start playing now**: the current single CTA, kept as the control.
- **Seat at the table**: hero shows a table with one open seat; CTA "Take your seat".
- **Live lobby**: a realistic table list ("Ark Nova · 3/4 players · starting soon") with Join buttons that lead to registration. The tables are illustrative, so label them "example tables".
- **Pick your vibe**: a 3-choice selector (Strategy / Family / With friends) that highlights matching games from the ten, then the CTA.
- **Invite your friends**: lead with "Play with your friends tonight: no setup, no cleanup", with a secondary "I have an invite" link.
- **Top ten shelf**: replace the 200-game wall with the ten games above as photo tiles; "See all 1,396 games" below.
- **Social proof hero**: the big numbers are the hero, with an animated count-up (static under reduced motion).
- **Three-step start**: Create account → Pick a game → Play in your browser. Answers the "is this hard?" doubt.
- **Ranked play**: lead with skill levels from beginner to pro and the ranking ladder.
- **Team up**: lead with playing on the same side or the same table: co-op games, "grab your crew", a team roll call with an open spot for you.
- **Open the box / Press start / Accept the quest / Roll for a game**: the theme's own verb on the primary CTA, with the plain "Create free account" beneath it so the action stays unambiguous.
