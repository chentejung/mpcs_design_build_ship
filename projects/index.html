    How to get a seat

    The process takes about as long as reading this column. Create a free account, choose one of the favourites above, and the table opens in your browser. Nothing to install, nothing to buy.

    The same account works on a computer, a tablet or a phone, so a game started on the sofa can be continued elsewhere. Games are available in 42 languages, with players from more than 200 countries across the board.

    Beginners and veterans share the same lobby. Pick a family game for a relaxed evening, a strategy title for a longer contest, or a co-operative one to face the danger together.
